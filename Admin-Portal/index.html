    APP CONTROLS

    App settings

    Manage credits, ads and image exports.

    New-user welcome credits

    Applies once when a new account is created, through email or Google. Existing accounts, logins and reinstalls receive no extra grant.

    Use 0 to disable welcome credits. Changes apply to account creations that start after the setting is saved.

    Ad placements

    Control existing Android placements without an app update. Requires the new Android build. Settings refresh about every five minutes while the app is open, or on return when due. Offline devices keep their last saved settings.

    One ad maximum per event and a shared minimum gap. Event ads require at least two eligible actions; automatic usage ads do not wait for navigation. Billing, account setup and other full-screen ads pause the timer. Generation and page loading continue behind the ad. Purchases and credit rewards are never delayed by an interstitial.

    Image watermark

    Add your logo to the bottom-right corner when users save or share a new creation. Requires the updated Android app. Existing gallery photos stay as saved.

    Transparent PNG · up to 2 MB. Uploaded to Cloudinary → picmee/branding/watermarks. Uploading alone does not change the live logo.

    Export preview

    Preview shows placement on a 3:2 image. On mobile, settings refresh at each save/share; offline exports use the last saved settings and logo.

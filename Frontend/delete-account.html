MADDS ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete your Madds account

You can request deletion of your Madds account and its associated data here, even if you no longer have the Android app installed. This page is for the Madds AI photo app (Android package: com.itsdhruvbhardwaj.picme).

Request account deletion by email ↗

The button opens your email app with a draft. Review it and press Send to submit your request. Opening this page or the email draft does not delete your account.

Or email madds.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Madds account.

1. Request deletion without the app

  1. Send your request to madds.support@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered with your Madds account. Include your registered email and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  2. Madds Support verifies account ownership before scheduling deletion. If you cannot access your registered email, explain this in your message so support can guide you through verification.
  3. Support will confirm the request and deletion schedule by email. After verification and scheduling, the account follows the 30-day grace period described below.

You do not need to install the app to use this email request option. Never send your password, one-time codes, password-reset links, or payment-card details.

2. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Madds → Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  2. Enter your current Madds password and complete the confirmation steps. If you signed in with Google and have not created a Madds password, use Create password in Settings first, or use the email option above.
  3. The app shows your scheduled deletion date and signs you out.

3. Timing and cancellation

Once deletion is scheduled, Madds provides a 30-day grace period. After that period, scheduled cleanup permanently removes the account and its associated Madds records. Deletion is not instantaneous when a request is sent.

If you change your mind before cleanup begins, use the app’s sign-in and cancel-deletion flow, or contact Madds Support from your registered email. Uninstalling Madds alone does not request account deletion.

4. Data deleted

Account cleanup removes your Madds profile and sign-in records, managed uploaded profile photos, saved preferences and favourites, credit balance and credit history, Madds purchase records, rewarded-ad records, generation history and temporary recovery results, notification registration and delivery records, feedback, and associated account audit records.

Your remaining credits and access to the deleted account are lost. Photos you already saved to your device’s gallery are not deleted remotely; you can remove those on your device.

5. Separate records and retention

Deleting a Madds account does not delete your Google account, remove Google Play’s own transaction records, or automatically issue a purchase refund. External service providers and backups may follow their own retention requirements, as explained in our Privacy Policy. Any information that must be kept for legal or security obligations is limited to what is necessary for those obligations.

Generated-image recovery copies have a separate maximum retention of 24 hours after completion; account deletion does not extend that period. If you have a question about data retained for your account, contact madds.support@gmail.com.

Need help?

Email madds.support@gmail.com for help with a Madds account-deletion request. You can also read our account help and Privacy Policy.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use the Madds Android app and website.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Contact

For questions about Madds or your information, email madds.support@gmail.com. For account-specific requests, use your registered email address so we can verify ownership.

2. Information we handle

We handle account details such as your name, email, verification status, sign-in information, and chosen avatar or profile photo. Service records include credit balances and transactions, verified purchases, favorites, generation style, time and status, and account activity. Google sign-in may provide your name, email, and profile image.

For notifications, we handle an app-installation identifier, notification registration information, device permission status, and category preferences. Support emails contain the information and attachments you choose to send. In-app feedback includes your message, category, account identifier, app version, Android version, and optional reply email. Authorized team members can review it to improve Madds and respond to requests. Hosting and delivery providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate their services.

3. Photos and AI generation

Selected photos and generation instructions are sent through Madds’s backend to the configured AI generation service. This may include OpenRouter routing requests to an image-model provider such as OpenAI. The app may resize and compress an upload copy while leaving your original gallery photo unchanged.

Generation records store service details such as style, status, time, and credits used; this is not a public photo gallery. Results are returned to the app for viewing and saving. Provider processing and retention depend on the service, routing, and account settings. See OpenRouter’s Privacy Policy and OpenAI API data controls. Do not upload images you do not have permission to use.

4. Why we use this information

We use information to operate and secure accounts, create images, maintain purchases and credit balances, show account history, deliver selected notifications, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. Authorised administrators can review account and service records, investigate problems, and make recorded account-access or credit adjustments.

5. Service providers

Madds uses Google Sign-In, Google Play, Google AdMob, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AI generation providers including OpenRouter and its configured model providers, and hosting, database, email, and image-delivery services. Cloudinary delivers catalogue images and stores uploaded profile photos. These services receive information needed to perform their functions and have their own privacy terms. Google Play processes payments; Madds does not receive your full payment-card details. We do not sell personal information.

6. Advertising, notifications, and the website

Google AdMob may process device, advertising, and usage information to deliver and measure ads and protect against fraud. Device and Google account settings provide relevant advertising controls. Notification delivery depends on device permission and in-app category preferences, which you can change in Settings. Generation results and download progress use separate device notification channels and are independent of optional promotional categories.

The website loads catalogue images from Cloudinary and may load fonts from Google Fonts. These external requests can share technical information such as your IP address with the content provider. Emailing our support address also shares your message with the email services used to send and receive it.

7. Retention and deletion

To recover interrupted generations, Madds temporarily stores generated results encrypted for up to 24 hours after completion. Access is restricted to the owning account. Recovery copies are scheduled for automatic deletion; transaction and generation audit records are retained separately. We keep account and service records while needed to operate Madds, investigate transactions, prevent abuse, and meet applicable obligations. Deletion requested in the app has a 30-day grace period. Follow the app’s cancellation flow during that period if you change your mind. After the grace period, scheduled cleanup removes the account and associated app records, including managed profile-photo assets. Provider records and backups may follow separate retention practices.

Abandoned email signups may be removed after 24 hours when the email is still unverified and there is no evidence of account use. This cleanup excludes Google accounts, verified accounts, and accounts with purchases or generation activity.

Visit our Madds account deletion page to request deletion without the app. You can also request deletion through the app or contact madds.support@gmail.com if you cannot access it. We may need to verify your identity. Information required for legal, security, or transaction-related reasons may be retained where applicable.

8. Your choices and security

You choose which photos to upload and whether to save generated results. You can manage your profile, notification categories, and account-deletion request through the app, and contact us about access or correction requests. We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Never share your password or verification codes with support.

9. Children and updates

Madds is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. We may update this policy as the service changes; the date above identifies this version.

Account & sign-in

I can’t sign in. What should I check?

Use the same email address or Google account you used to create your Madds account. Check your connection and whether your email needs verification. If the problem continues, email support with the message shown on screen.

I haven’t received my verification or password-reset email.

Check the email address you entered, then look in Spam and Promotions. Use the most recent link requested for that account. Never send your password, verification code, or reset link to support.

How do I reset or create an app password?

Use the password options available in the app’s account settings. If you signed up with Google and have not created an app password, create one before using password reset. You can also use Forgot password on the sign-in screen when your account has a password.

Creating images

How do I get better results?

Choose a clear, well-lit photo and follow the style’s required photo count. Keep faces unobstructed and include clothing or pose details the style needs. Only upload photos you have permission to use. AI results can vary between attempts.

My generation failed or credits seem incorrect.

Check Credit History and your balance. Confirmed failed generations can receive a credit refund; a request with an uncertain outcome may need review. Before retrying a stalled request, check whether it completed. Email support with the style name, approximate time, and a screenshot if the issue remains.

Where can I find or save a result?

Use the result screen’s save option after generation. Check the app’s available generation history and your device gallery. Keeping a saved copy on your device is the best way to retain an image you want to use later.

Credits & purchases

What are credits used for?

Credits are used to create images in Madds. Check the style’s displayed credit cost before generating. You can review balance changes in Credit History and purchases in My Purchases.

I paid but my credits haven’t appeared.

Make sure the Google Play purchase completed and that Madds is signed in to the account used for the purchase. Reopen the app while online and check My Purchases. If credits are still missing, email support with your Madds account email, purchase date, pack name, and Google Play order reference. Do not send payment-card details.

How do I request a payment refund?

Payment refunds are different from restoring app credits. Check Google Play’s refund process and contact us if you need help identifying a purchase. Eligibility depends on the purchase and applicable Google Play policies; sending a support request does not guarantee a refund.

A rewarded ad didn’t add a credit.

Allow the rewarded ad to finish and keep an internet connection while the reward is verified. Check Credit History before watching another ad. If the reward is missing, include the approximate time and app version in your support email.

Notifications

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check both Android’s notification permission for Madds and the category switches inside Madds Settings → Notifications. Open Madds while signed in and online to let the device registration sync. Notification availability also depends on the selected audience, account activity, and delivery limits.

Can I turn off only some notifications?

Yes. Use Settings → Notifications in Madds to manage available categories. Android’s system notification settings let you manage delivery on that device.

Privacy & deletion

How do I delete my account?

Use Delete account in Madds Settings and follow the confirmation steps. Account deletion has a 30-day grace period. If you cannot access the app, email madds.support@gmail.com from your registered email address to request help. We may need to verify ownership before handling an account request.

Where can I read about my data?

Our Privacy Policy explains account information, photos, AI providers, advertising, notifications, and retention.
